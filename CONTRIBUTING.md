# Contributing to Early Pancreatic Cancer Detection

## Team Workflow

1. Do not directly modify the main branch.
2. Create or use your assigned feature branch.
3. Make changes only related to your assigned module.
4. Test your changes before pushing.
5. Commit with a clear message.
6. Push your branch.
7. Create a Pull Request.
8. Another team member reviews the changes.
9. Merge into main only after review.

## Branches

- feature/data-preprocessing
- feature/ct-model
- feature/multimodal-fusion
- feature/application

## Important Rules

- Do not upload medical datasets.
- Do not upload patient-identifiable information.
- Do not upload passwords or API keys.
- Do not fabricate experimental results.
- Do not change another member's module without discussion.
